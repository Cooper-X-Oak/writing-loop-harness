# 10_hotspot_scan Rules

This node scans for AI/frontier-tech hotspot candidates. It does not write articles.

- Prefer 6-hour community traction over papers, benchmarks, and method-name news.
- Treat paper/RSS/arXiv-only items as background material unless they already have community or product impact.
- Do not fetch private account data, cookies, or platform backends.
- Save candidate evidence as artifacts; do not publish or post externally.
