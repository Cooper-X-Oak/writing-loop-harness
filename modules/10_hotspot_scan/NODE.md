# 10_hotspot_scan

## Purpose

Build a short candidate list of fresh AI/technology hotspots with visible discussion, conflict, product impact, or share potential.

## Enter When

- A new writing cycle starts.
- The prior cycle finished or paused without a usable topic.
- The human asks to refresh hotspot candidates.

## Steps

1. Read `CONTRACT.md` and source policy.
2. Scan configured public sources or supplied candidate files.
3. Separate candidates into community-hot, media/product-impact, and paper-only buckets.
4. Downweight paper-only candidates to background material.
5. Produce a candidate list with evidence, time window, source URLs, and preliminary interaction signal.

## Exit

Route according to `GATE.md`.
