# 10_hotspot_scan Gate

## Outcomes

- pass
- pause
- fail

## Pass

Pass when at least one non-duplicate candidate has evidence of recent community discussion, product impact, controversy, or clear share potential.

## Pause

Pause when:

- all candidates are paper-only or benchmark-only;
- all candidates are older than the configured freshness window;
- source access is partial and human should choose whether to use fallback;
- candidate quality is too weak for the daily publish budget.

## Fail

Fail only when no candidate source can be read and no supplied candidate artifact exists.
