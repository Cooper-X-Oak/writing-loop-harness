# 20_topic_gate Contract

## Inputs

- `10_hotspot_scan` candidate artifact.
- Existing duplicate registry or prior topic list.
- Daily publish budget state.

## Outputs

- Selected topic artifact with:
  - chosen title;
  - reason for selection;
  - rejected alternatives;
  - evidence of interaction potential;
  - duplicate check;
  - paper-source decision;
  - daily budget decision.

## Artifacts

- `.runs/<run-id>/nodes/20_topic_gate/selected-topic.md`
- `.runs/<run-id>/nodes/20_topic_gate/topic-scorecard.json`

## Dependencies

- None required.
