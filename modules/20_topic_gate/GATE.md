# 20_topic_gate Gate

## Outcomes

- pass
- pause
- fail

## Pass

Pass when exactly one topic is selected and the scorecard shows why it deserves the cycle.

## Pause

Pause when:

- no topic clears the interaction threshold;
- the best topic is paper-only;
- two topics are close and require human editorial judgment;
- daily budget is already consumed.

## Fail

Fail only when candidate artifacts are missing or structurally unreadable.
